About us

The Grêmio Hockey Club is a sports club based in Porto Alegre, Brazil. Founded in 2018, the club has quickly established itself as a prominent force in the world of hockey. With a strong commitment to excellence and a passion for the sport, the Grêmio Hockey Club offers top-notch training programs, competitive teams, and a vibrant community for hockey enthusiasts of all ages.

The club's mission is to promote the sport of hockey, foster talent development, and create a supportive environment for players to thrive. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, the Grêmio Hockey Club welcomes you to join their ranks and be part of an exciting journey in the world of hockey.
